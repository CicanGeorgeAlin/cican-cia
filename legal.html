LEGAL · INDEPENDENCE · SOURCE POLICY

INDEPENDENT PROJECT

CICAN is an independent educational and research project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, approved by, or operated by the U.S. Central Intelligence Agency, the U.S. Government, or any other government agency.

1. PURPOSE

CICAN provides an educational research and simulation experience using publicly available information and public/declassified government records. Simulated ranks, access stages, compartments, missions, rewards, investigations, and gameplay mechanics are fictional elements of the CICAN experience. They do not grant, represent, reproduce, or imply real security clearance, government authority, employment, authorization, or access to classified information.

2. USE OF CIA REFERENCES

The terms “Central Intelligence Agency” and “CIA” may appear where necessary to identify the actual government agency, historical record, collection, or source being discussed. Such references are factual source identification and are not intended to imply sponsorship, endorsement, authorization, partnership, or affiliation.

CICAN does not use the official CIA seal or other official CIA insignia as its brand identity. CICAN's product identity is solely “CICAN.”

3. SOURCE POLICY

Where possible, CICAN links users directly to the original public government record or official government source. Users should treat the linked government source as the authoritative source for the underlying document. CICAN summaries, descriptions, questions, simulations, and educational interpretations are separate from the government's source material.

Source descriptions are intended to distinguish documented facts from simulation. CICAN does not claim that simulated access labels are the actual access requirements for any government record.

4. CLASSIFICATION & ACCESS

CICAN distinguishes formal classification levels from controlled-access concepts. Its gameplay may use labels such as CONFIDENTIAL, SECRET, TOP SECRET, SCI, SI, TK, or GAMMA as educational or historical representations. These labels must not be interpreted as granting real access or describing a user's actual eligibility or clearance.

5. COPYRIGHT & GOVERNMENT SOURCES

Government-source material may have its own copyright, credit, image, or other usage conditions. CICAN does not assume that every item hosted on an external government website is unrestricted merely because it is publicly accessible. Third-party material remains subject to its applicable rights and terms.

6. PRIVACY

The current CICAN website is designed to operate without requiring an account. Gameplay progress and profile statistics may be stored locally in the user's browser using localStorage. The current project does not intentionally send those gameplay statistics to CICAN servers. External government links may have their own privacy practices, which are outside CICAN's control.

If future versions introduce accounts, analytics, advertising, payments, newsletters, cookies, or other collection of personal information, this policy must be reviewed and updated before those features are deployed.

7. EXTERNAL LINKS

CICAN links to external websites, including official government sources. CICAN does not control those websites, their availability, their content, or their privacy practices. A link does not constitute endorsement or affiliation.

8. NO PROFESSIONAL OR GOVERNMENT ADVICE

CICAN is educational software. It is not legal advice, intelligence-community training, security-clearance advice, government advice, or professional intelligence instruction. Users should consult an appropriately qualified professional when a real-world legal or professional decision requires advice.

9. ACCURACY

CICAN aims to use evidence-based public sources, but historical records, metadata, websites, and external links can change or contain errors. Users should verify important claims against the original source.

10. BRANDING

CICAN is the independent project and product identity. The project does not present itself as the CIA or as an official government product.

11. LEGAL REVIEW NOTICE

This page is a practical project disclosure and source-policy statement, not a legal opinion. Laws and platform requirements can vary by jurisdiction and can change. Before commercial distribution, paid features, or publication through an app store, CICAN should receive a jurisdiction-specific legal review covering branding, trademarks, consumer protection, privacy/data protection, copyright, and platform rules.
