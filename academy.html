PUBLIC RECORD INTELLIGENCE · LEARNING SIMULATION

A progressive research experience built from publicly available CIA material. You begin as a recruit and develop the skills needed to navigate a large historical archive: terminology, context, query construction, source evaluation, and evidence connection.

IMPORTANT: This is an independent educational simulation. It is not CIA training, does not represent CIA hiring requirements, does not provide security clearance, and does not provide access to classified information. All simulated ranks and access states belong to CICAN CIA.

03 · THE SOURCE LIBRARY

CIA FOIA ELECTRONIC READING ROOM

The curriculum is built around public historical material. CIA describes the Reading Room as a public database containing recently declassified documents across many subjects, including historical collections and original document images.

04 · THE PRINCIPLE

DO NOT GUESS. FIND EVIDENCE.

If the public record does not establish an answer, the correct research outcome is to identify the gap. CICAN rewards careful sourcing, not confident speculation.
