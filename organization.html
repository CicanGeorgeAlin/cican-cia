Before you follow an access path, understand the organization that produces and integrates intelligence. The public CIA model separates career disciplines into five directorates and brings them together through Mission Centers.

This map mirrors the CIA's publicly documented organization. It does not reproduce non-public internal structures. Everything beyond the public record is represented only as CICAN simulation.
